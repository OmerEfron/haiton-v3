# Haiton

A social app where people share short articles with their connections. An AI interviews the author about what happened and writes the article for them.

## Language

### Writing

**Article**:
A published piece, written by one author and shown to an audience.
_Avoid_: Story, post

**Draft**:
Unpublished article content written by the Editor from an Interview.
_Avoid_: Story

**Interview**:
The conversation between a user and the Reporter about something that happened to them.
_Avoid_: Chat, session

**Reporter**:
The AI that asks the questions in an Interview.
_Avoid_: Bot, assistant

**Editor**:
The AI that writes a Draft from an Interview.
_Avoid_: Writer, generator

**Fact**:
Something the Reporter remembers about a user and uses in later Interviews. Shown to the user under the Hebrew name כרטסת.
_Avoid_: Memory, note

**Flash**:
The one-line teaser of an Article, shown on the ticker.
_Avoid_: Snippet, teaser

**Push**:
The optional notification text sent when an Article is published.
_Avoid_: Alert

### Sharing

**Connection**:
A link between two users, held as one row per side so each side has its own mute.
_Avoid_: Friend, follow

**Circle**:
All of a user's Connections.
_Avoid_: Friends list, network

**Audience**:
Who an Article is shown to: the Circle, only the author, chosen people, or a group.
_Avoid_: Recipients, visibility

**Visible**:
An Article is visible to a user if they wrote it, or they are in its Audience and still connected to the author. Removing the Connection hides the Article.
_Avoid_: Accessible, shared with
